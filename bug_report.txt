Title: Complaints flagged for human review are being auto-routed

A support lead noticed that a complaint the triage step had flagged for human
review went straight to the automated acknowledgement queue instead of a person.
Example: a triage result with category "damaged", priority "P1", a one-line
summary, and needs_human true produced an intake record with auto_route true.

Observed in the training environment with Python 3.12.
No exception is shown.
