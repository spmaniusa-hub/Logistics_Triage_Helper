# Final Project Rubric - 100 Points

- Understanding of concepts: 15
- Prompt quality and technique: 25
- Structured output and validation: 20
- Problem-solving and troubleshooting: 15
- Verification and professional judgement: 15
- Documentation and presentation: 10

Full marks require visible prompts, reasons for technique choices, evidence across 10 inputs, a real diagnosed failure, all four verification steps, and a genuine boundary where AI was not appropriate.
